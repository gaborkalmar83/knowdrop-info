Daily trivia quiz: the Daily Drop

The Daily Drop is the daily trivia quiz at the heart of Knowdrop. Once a day, everyone gets the same 10 questions, so your score means something when you compare it with friends.

What arrives every day

Every day at 00:00 UTC a new Daily Drop arrives. It holds 10 trivia questions, from 3 easy to 3 hard, so the quiz warms you up and then asks something harder. In the Netherlands, 00:00 UTC is 01:00 in winter and 02:00 in summer. Wherever you live, the new Drop appears at that same moment for every player.

Because everyone gets the same 10, a daily score is easy to compare. You can ask a friend how they did on today's Drop and know that you both answered exactly the same questions. If you close the app by accident, you continue at the same question.

Streaks and the weekly freeze

Play the Daily Drop on consecutive days to build a streak. Everyone misses a day sometimes, so you get one free streak freeze every week. It covers a missed day and keeps your streak alive.

Finishing Daily Drops also earns sticker packs for the sticker album. Complete a page of the album to unlock an avatar frame. Stickers are earned by playing, never sold.

Share your result without spoilers

When you finish, you can share your result as an emoji score card. It shows how you did without giving away any of the questions, so a friend who has not played yet is not spoiled. The statistics screen keeps your recent Daily Drops, your best scores and your accuracy per category.

Questions you can check

Every question in the Daily Drop is written with the help of AI and then checked by a separate, independent fact-check against reliable sources. After each answer you see a fun fact and a source link, and if you spot a mistake you can tap Report. The page on how we fact-check explains the steps.

Reminders only if you want them

A daily reminder is optional. If you switch it on, you get at most 2 a day and never at night. The Daily Drop is free to play, works offline, and has no lives, no energy and no waiting timers. If you would rather start with a picture round, try the flags quiz.

Questions and answers

When does the new Daily Drop arrive?

Every day at 00:00 UTC a new Daily Drop arrives: 10 questions, from 3 easy to 3 hard. Everyone gets the same 10, so you can compare scores with friends.

What happens to my streak if I miss a day?

You keep a streak by playing, and one free streak freeze per week covers a missed day.

Can I share my Daily Drop result?

Yes. You can share your result as an emoji score card, without spoilers.
