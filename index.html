Knowdrop: the daily trivia quiz for Android

A fresh Daily Drop of 10 fact-checked questions every day, picture quizzes, and battles with friends. No energy, no timers, and it works offline.

Free to play. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

What is Knowdrop?

Knowdrop is a trivia game for Android that gives you something new every day. Play the Daily Drop, work through quick rounds in 13 categories, race the clock in Speed Run, or see how far you get in Sudden Death. Then challenge friends on one phone or on their own phones. It is made by Kalmarium Services BV in the Netherlands.

What you get

Daily Drop

Every day at 00:00 UTC a new Drop arrives: 10 questions, from 3 easy to 3 hard. Everyone gets the same 10, so you can compare scores with friends. Keep your streak going, and use a free streak freeze if you miss a day.

13 categories and themed packs

More than 12,000 questions across General Knowledge, History, Science, Geography, Movies and TV, Music, Sports and more, plus themed packs such as Animals, Space, Olympics and Dinosaurs. New questions arrive by themselves, with no app update.

Picture Reveal

Guess flags, landmarks, animals and cars as a pixelated picture comes into focus. The sooner you answer, the more you score.

Play with friends

Pass and play with 2 to 4 players on one phone, host an online game that friends join with a code, QR code or PIN, send a challenge code over WhatsApp, and compare scores on daily and weekly leaderboards.

Fact-checked with a fun fact

Questions are written with AI help and then checked by a separate fact-check against reliable sources. Every answer shows a fun fact and a source link, and you can report any question you think is wrong.

Streaks, stickers and trophies

Earn sticker packs for finishing Daily Drops and winning, complete album pages to unlock avatar frames, and collect trophies. Stickers are earned, never sold.

Questions and answers

Is Knowdrop free?

Yes, Knowdrop is free to play. There are no lives, no energy and no waiting timers. Free players see a small banner ad on menu and result screens, never during a question or a match and not in the first 3 days. Optional purchases (Remove ads forever, or the Plus subscription) turn ads off.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. The whole game works offline, so you can play on a plane or a train. Online features such as friends, leaderboards and live parties need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to play. If you pick a nickname and an avatar, the app creates a simple online account for friends and leaderboards, without an email address or a password. You can delete it in the app at any time.

How are the questions made and checked?

Questions are written with the help of AI and then checked by a separate, independent fact-check against reliable sources. After each answer you see a fun fact and a source link. If you spot a mistake, tap Report on the question and we will look at it.

Can I play with friends on their own phones?

Yes. Host an online game and friends join from their own phones with a room code, a QR code, a PIN or an open game. You can also play pass and play with 2 to 4 players on one phone.
