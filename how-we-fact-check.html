How every trivia question is written and fact-checked

A trivia game is only fun if the answers are right. This page explains in plain words how Knowdrop questions are made, what checks them, and what you can do when you think one is wrong.

AI helps to write the questions

We say it up front: Knowdrop questions are written with the help of AI. The question bank holds more than 12,000 questions in 13 categories, plus 12 themed packs. That is far more than one person could write by hand, and AI makes the first draft possible. A draft is never the end of the process.

A separate fact-check

After a question is drafted, a separate, independent fact-check goes over it against reliable sources. The check is a separate step from the writing. A question that does not pass is fixed or replaced.

A fun fact and a source link on every answer

You do not have to take our word for it. After each answer, Knowdrop shows a fun fact and a source link, so you can see where the answer comes from and read more. A fun fact is a small reward for a right answer and a gentle explanation after a wrong one.

Mistakes still happen, so there is a Report button

We do not claim that a question is never wrong. Facts change, sources disagree, and a checked question can still contain an error. That is why every question has a Report button. Tap Report on the question and we will look at it. You can also email kalmariumservices@gmail.com.

Suggest a topic or a question

Players can suggest a topic or a full question from inside the app. Good ideas are reviewed and fact-checked before they can become part of the game. The app also offers a "Suggested for you" list, which picks topics based on what you play.

New questions without an update

New questions and pictures arrive by themselves, so you do not need to update the app to get fresh trivia. The Daily Drop uses the same question bank, and the flags quiz uses pictures from it.
